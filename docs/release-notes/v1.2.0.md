## Meshwise 1.2.0: Smart measuring

Measuring no longer means tapping two loose points and hoping they landed in the right place. Tap a **feature** and Meshwise works out what it is, even in a plain STL file.

**One tap:**
- **Hole or shaft:** diameter, radius and depth
- **Round edge** (hole rim, arc): diameter and center
- **Flat face:** area and which way it faces
- **Edge:** length · **Corner:** position

**Two taps** give the measurement that makes sense:
- Two parallel faces → the distance between them (wall thickness, gaps, slots)
- Two faces at an angle → the angle
- Two holes → center distance and the wall between them, or "concentric"
- A hole and a face → axis-to-face distance and the material left to the hole
- Anything else → point to point with ΔX / ΔY / ΔZ

**Also new:**
- **Precision cursor:** hold and drag; a crosshair floats above your finger and shows what it will pick
- What you picked is outlined on the model, with a dimension line and value
- **Keep** measurements on screen while you take the next one, and copy them as text
- The distance, angle and 3-point circle modes now snap to corners and edges
- A public roadmap: [docs/ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/doesthings/Meshwise/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md)

> **Honest note:** Meshwise is vibe coded: I built it with a lot of help from an AI coding assistant. It works well enough, and I just wanted to share a good free tool with whoever needs one. Bug reports are very welcome, especially models where a hole, face or edge isn't recognised.
