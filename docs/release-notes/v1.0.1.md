## Meshwise 1.0.1

A small update to 1.0.0. It installs over it and keeps your files, projects and settings.

**Update check (optional):** Meshwise can now tell you when a new version is out. On first launch it asks whether it may check GitHub for updates, and it stays off unless you say yes. When on, it asks GitHub for the latest version number at most once a day and sends nothing else; you decide whether to download. You can also tap **Check now** in Settings. This is the app's only network use, so 1.0.1 adds the internet permission for it.

**New icon:** a faceted gem with an orange section cut, in the app's own colors. It also has a themed version for Android 13+.

**Smoother:**
- Opening a very large model no longer freezes touch input while the model is sent to the GPU.

**More robust:**
- Fixed a possible crash when a touch or a background task arrived just after the viewer closed.

**Clearer text:**
- Shorter, plainer wording in the app (measuring hints, warnings, empty library, About).
- The home screen now lists OFF among the supported formats.

New to Meshwise? It's a fast, free and open-source 3D model viewer for Android: STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, OFF, STEP, IGES, BREP and ZIP, with section cuts, measuring, wall thickness, assemblies and weight estimates. No ads, no account, no tracking.

> **Honest note:** Meshwise is vibe coded: I built it with a lot of help from an AI coding assistant. It works well enough, and I just wanted to share a good free tool with whoever needs one. Bug reports are very welcome.
