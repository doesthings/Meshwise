## Meshwise 1.3.0: Print prep

Lots of files arrive in the wrong orientation or the wrong size. The new **Print** tool fixes that before you send the model to your slicer. It replaces the Weight button, so the bottom bar stays as tidy as before; weight is one of its tabs.

**Orient**
- **Tap a face** and the model turns so that face lies flat on the bed
- **Suggest** compares the model's sides and large flat faces and offers the best three, ranked by overhang area, contact with the bed and height
- Live numbers for the current orientation: overhangs, height and area on the bed, with the overhang shading one tap away
- Rotate in 90° steps, mirror, undo, or go back to the file as it was

**Size**
- Scale by percent, or set one side to an exact size
- One-tap fixes for files exported in inches, centimeters or meters

**Printer**
- Pick your printer (Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Elegoo) or enter a custom size
- The build volume is drawn around the model: fits, fits when turned 90°, or too big, with **Scale to fit**

Exports and projects keep the new orientation and size.

**Updates now show what changed**
- When a new version is out, Meshwise shows what's new in every version you missed
- **Update** downloads it from GitHub, checks it against the release checksums and installs it over the old one (Android asks you to confirm). Your files, projects and settings stay
- The once-a-day check is now on by default and can be turned off in Settings
