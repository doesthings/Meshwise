## Meshwise 1.3.1: Print estimate

How long will it take, and how much filament? The Print tool's new **Estimate** tab works that out by slicing the model the way a slicer does, with your printer's usual slicer settings.

**What you get**
- Print time, filament length and weight, and cost
- Where the time goes: walls, infill, top and bottom layers, supports, travel
- A heads-up when overhangs need supports, with a one-tap fix

**What you can change**
- Layer height, infill (lines, grid, cubic or gyroid), walls, and normal or tree supports
- Material (PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, nylon, PC) and 1.75 or 2.85 mm filament
- Pick your printer in the Printer tab: Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality and Elegoo presets carry their slicer's defaults

**How close is it?** In tests against Bambu Studio's own estimates (92 slices of 10 models on 4 printers), print times differed by 3.4% on average and filament by 1.9% without supports. Supports are rougher (about 20% with normal supports). Heating, homing and bed levelling aren't included. Details are in the README.

Version numbers now go up more slowly: most updates raise only the last number.
