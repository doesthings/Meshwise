## Meshwise 1.1.0

A redesign of the whole interface, and a lot more ways to make Meshwise your own. It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your files, projects and settings.

**New look:** a floating dock at the bottom, a compact tool cluster at the top, cleaner tool panels, and redesigned home, settings and about screens. Recent files show as a grid of large thumbnails or as a list, with search and sorting.

**Make it yours:**
- Customize the toolbar: pick which tools sit in the bottom bar, which in the top cluster and which are hidden, and set their order
- Eight accent colors (or Material You), and a pure black mode for OLED screens
- Button size, panel opacity, button labels and a left-handed layout
- Opening view, field of view, model color and material for files without colors, selection highlight color
- Five new backgrounds plus your own two-color gradient
- Invert drag, choose what a double tap does, turntable speed and direction, haptic feedback
- Measurement decimal places, reopen the last model on start, and reset all settings

**Fixes:**
- The first model you open is no longer framed too large and cut off at the sides
- On some screen widths, the fit view button overlapped the tool buttons
- Page titles are readable in the dark theme

**Also new:** transparent PNG export, and quick standard views (Iso, Front, Top, …) in the View panel.

> **Honest note:** Meshwise is vibe coded: I built it with a lot of help from an AI coding assistant. It works well enough, and I just wanted to share a good free tool with whoever needs one. Bug reports are very welcome.
