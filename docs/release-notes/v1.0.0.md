## Meshwise 1.0.0

**Meshwise** is a fast, free and open-source 3D model viewer for Android. It's built for checking 3D prints and CAD parts on your phone.

**Open almost anything:** STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, OFF, STEP, IGES, BREP and ZIP archives, straight from Files, Drive, email or any app that shares a model.

**Inspect:**
- Section cuts with hatched caps
- Wall-thickness heat map with thinnest-wall callouts
- Overhang view and mesh check
- Measure distance, circle (radius, diameter, center), angle and point coordinates
- Weight, filament and cost estimates for 3D printing

**Assemblies:** parts list, exploded view with a drag gizmo, x-ray, per-part colors and materials.

**Save and share:** projects, plus export to STL, OBJ, 3MF and PNG.

No ads, no account, no tracking. The app does not even have network permission.

> Honest note: Meshwise is vibe coded (built with a lot of help from an AI coding assistant). It works well enough, and I just wanted to share a good free tool with whoever needs one. Bug reports are very welcome.
