## Meshwise 1.0.0

**Meshwise** is a fast, free and open-source 3D model viewer for Android. Use it to check 3D prints and CAD parts on your phone.

**Opens:** STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, OFF, STEP, IGES, BREP and ZIP archives. Open them in the app, or from Files, Drive, email or any app that can share a file.

**Inspect:**
- Section cuts with hatched caps
- Wall-thickness heat map that points out the thinnest walls
- Overhang view and mesh check
- Measure distances, circles (radius, diameter, center), angles and points
- Weight, filament and cost estimates for 3D printing

**Assemblies:** parts list, exploded view with a drag gizmo, x-ray, and colors and materials per part.

**Save and share:** projects, plus export to STL, OBJ, 3MF and PNG.

No ads, no account, no tracking. The app doesn't even have internet permission.
